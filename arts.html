About This Career Field

Arts and Humanities help students understand people, society, culture, history and human expression. This field offers diverse career opportunities in education, communication, social sciences, media and public services.

Education Path

Students can choose undergraduate and postgraduate programs in subjects such as Arts, Humanities, Languages, History, Political Science, Sociology and Psychology. Further education can lead to specialized careers and research opportunities.
