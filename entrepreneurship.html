Entrepreneurship

Explore entrepreneurship, startups, business ideas, innovation and opportunities to build your own venture.

About This Career Field

Entrepreneurship involves identifying problems, creating solutions and building products or services that provide value to customers. Entrepreneurs can start businesses, develop innovative ideas and create employment opportunities.

  • Entrepreneur

  • Business Owner

  • Business Development Professional
  • Innovation Manager
  • Product Manager
  • Small Business Manager

Education Path

There is no single required degree to become an entrepreneur. Students can study business, management, engineering, commerce, technology or other fields and develop entrepreneurial skills through projects, internships and practical experience.
