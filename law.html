Law

Explore careers related to law, justice, legal services and the judicial system.

About This Career Field

Law is a field focused on understanding and applying rules, regulations and legal principles. Legal professionals help individuals, businesses and organizations understand their rights and responsibilities.

Popular Areas of Law

  • Corporate Law
  • Criminal Law
  • Civil Law
  • Constitutional Law
  • Family Law
  • Intellectual Property Law
  • Cyber Law

Important Skills

  • Communication Skills
  • Critical Thinking
  • Research Skills
  • Logical Reasoning
  • Problem Solving
  • Negotiation Skills
  • Public Speaking

Career Opportunities

  • Lawyer / Advocate
  • Legal Advisor
  • Corporate Lawyer
  • Legal Consultant
  • Public Prosecutor
  • Judicial Services
  • Legal Researcher
  • Legal Compliance Professional

Education Path

Students can pursue law through undergraduate programs such as integrated law courses after Class 12 or a law degree after graduation. Further specialization can be pursued in areas such as corporate law, criminal law, cyber law and intellectual property law.
